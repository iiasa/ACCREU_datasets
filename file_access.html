--- CWatM RCP4.5 Hydrological Data – Daily and Monthly Outputs ---

This page provides direct access to hydrological simulation outputs from the Community Water Model (CWatM) under the RCP4.5 climate scenario. Daily outputs contain total runoff (QTot), while monthly outputs contain total runoff (QTot) and river discharge (Dis).

Expand the folders below and click the links to access the corresponding data files.
